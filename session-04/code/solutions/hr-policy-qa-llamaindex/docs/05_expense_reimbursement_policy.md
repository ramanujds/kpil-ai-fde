# Expense Reimbursement Policy
Department: Finance
Version: 2025
Effective: 1 April 2025

## What Can Be Claimed
Employees can claim reasonable expenses incurred for company work: travel, accommodation, meals while travelling, client entertainment and approved training. Personal expenses, fines, alcohol and expenses for family members cannot be claimed.

## Submitting a Claim
Claims are submitted in the expense portal with the original receipt or invoice attached. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Claims older than 30 days are not paid, unless the department head approves an exception in writing. Claims without a receipt are not paid, except for local travel of up to Rs 200 per trip.

## Approval and Payment
The reporting manager approves the claim first. Claims above Rs 25,000 also need approval from the department head. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary if approved by the 20th of the month. Claims approved after the 20th are paid the following month.

## Travel Limits
Employees travelling within India for work can book economy-class flights, or AC 2-tier on trains. Hotel stays are limited to Rs 6,000 per night in metro cities and Rs 4,000 per night elsewhere. Managers and above may stay up to Rs 8,000 per night in metro cities. Travel must be booked through the company travel desk at least 5 days before the trip.

## Meal Allowance
While travelling, employees can claim meals up to Rs 1,200 per day within India. Meals while working at the office are not claimable. Client meals can be claimed up to Rs 1,500 per person, with the client's name and purpose written on the claim.

## Mobile and Internet
A monthly mobile allowance of Rs 500 is paid to all employees. Employees whose roles need heavy phone use, such as sales, can claim up to Rs 1,500 per month against the bill.

## Advances
An advance of up to 80 percent of the estimated trip cost can be requested through the expense portal at least 3 days before travel. The balance must be settled within 7 days of returning, with receipts. Unsettled advances are deducted from the next salary.

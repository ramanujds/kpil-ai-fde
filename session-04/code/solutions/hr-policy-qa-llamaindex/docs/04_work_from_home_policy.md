# Work From Home Policy
Department: HR
Version: 2025
Effective: 1 April 2025

## Hybrid Model
Acme follows a hybrid model. Employees work from the office for 3 days a week and from home for 2 days. The office days are set by each team, so that team members are in on the same days. Customer-facing and site-based roles follow the schedule set by their manager.

## Eligibility
Work from home is available after the employee has completed probation. Employees on probation work from the office full time, unless the manager approves a one-off request. Roles that need physical presence, such as facilities and reception, are not eligible.

## Working Remotely From Another City
An employee who wants to work from a different city for a longer period, for example to care for a family member, can request it for up to 4 weeks in a year. The request needs approval from the manager and HR, and the employee must be reachable during core hours. Working from outside India is not permitted without HR and Legal approval, for tax and compliance reasons.

## Home Internet and Equipment
Employees are responsible for a stable internet connection at home. A monthly internet allowance of Rs 800 is reimbursed against the bill for employees who work from home at least 2 days a week. Company laptops must be used for all work.

## Availability and Meetings
Employees working from home must be reachable on chat and email during core hours. Cameras are expected to be on for team meetings unless there is a connection problem. Time off during a work-from-home day must be applied for as leave.

## Security While Remote
Work from home only on a trusted network. Public Wi-Fi must be used only with the company VPN turned on. Screens must be locked when stepping away, and company documents must not be visible to others in the household.

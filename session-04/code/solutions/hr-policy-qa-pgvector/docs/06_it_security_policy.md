# IT Security Policy
Department: IT
Version: 2025
Effective: 1 April 2025

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include letters, numbers and a symbol. Passwords must be changed every 90 days and must not repeat any of the last 5 passwords. Never share a password with anyone, including IT staff. Use the company-approved password manager to store passwords, rather than writing them down.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the HR portal, the expense portal, the VPN and all cloud tools. The MFA app must be set up on the first day of joining. If a phone with the MFA app is lost, report it to the IT service desk straight away so access can be reset.

## VPN and Remote Access
The company VPN must be used when connecting to company systems from outside the office, and always on public Wi-Fi. The VPN disconnects automatically after 12 hours and must be reconnected.

## Phishing and Suspicious Emails
Do not click links or open attachments in emails you did not expect. Report a suspicious email using the Report Phishing button in the email client, or forward it to security@acme-example.com. If you have already clicked a suspicious link or entered your password, tell IT immediately. Reporting quickly is never penalised.

## Data Handling
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data must not be sent to personal email, saved on personal devices, or shared on unapproved tools. Customer data must never be pasted into public AI tools. Only the AI tools approved by IT may be used with company information.

## Reporting a Security Incident
Any suspected security incident, such as a lost device, a leaked document or an unauthorised login, must be reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours. Call the 24x7 helpline on the number printed on the back of the access card for urgent cases. Do not try to investigate or fix the incident on your own.

## Software Installation
Only software from the approved list may be installed on company devices. A request to add new software goes through the IT service desk and is reviewed within 5 working days. Software with unclear licensing is not approved.

# HR Handbook
Department: HR
Version: 2025
Effective: 1 April 2025

## Working Hours
The standard working week is Monday to Friday. Core hours are 10:30 am to 4:30 pm, and every employee is expected to be available during them. The remaining hours can be arranged with the reporting manager. Total working time is 8 hours a day, including a 45-minute lunch break.

## Probation
Every new employee serves a probation period of 6 months. Performance is reviewed at the end of month 3 and month 6. Probation may be extended once, by up to 3 months, if the manager and HR agree that more time is needed. Confirmation is communicated in writing by HR.

## Notice Period
The notice period is 30 days for employees on probation and 60 days for confirmed employees. Managers and above serve 90 days. The company may agree to a shorter notice period, or a buy-out of the remaining days, at the discretion of the department head and HR.

## Performance Reviews
Performance is reviewed twice a year, in April and in October. Each review has a self-assessment, a manager assessment and a calibration meeting. Ratings use a five-point scale. Salary revisions and promotions are decided once a year, after the October review.

## Grievance Redressal
An employee with a workplace concern should first speak to their reporting manager. If the matter is not resolved within 10 working days, or if the concern involves the manager, the employee can write to hr-grievance@acme-example.com. HR acknowledges every grievance within 2 working days and aims to close it within 21 days. Complaints are handled confidentially and retaliation against anyone raising a concern is prohibited.

## Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers and vendors with respect. Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated. Gifts from vendors worth more than Rs 2,000 must be declared to HR. Employees must not use company information for personal gain or share it outside the company without approval.

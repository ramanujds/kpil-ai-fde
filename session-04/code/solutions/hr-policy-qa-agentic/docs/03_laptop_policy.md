# Laptop and Equipment Policy
Department: IT
Version: 2025
Effective: 1 April 2025

## Who Gets a Laptop
Every full-time employee is issued one company laptop on the first day of joining. Interns and contractors get a laptop only if their manager raises a request and the department head approves it. Employees on the engineering and design teams receive a high-performance model. All other employees receive a standard model.

## Refresh Cycle
Laptops are replaced every 3 years. A laptop may be replaced earlier if it is damaged beyond repair or no longer meets the needs of the role, with IT approval. Old laptops must be returned to IT when the replacement is issued.

## Acceptable Use
The laptop is for company work. Limited personal use, such as browsing or personal email, is allowed as long as it does not affect work or break the IT Security Policy. Employees must not install pirated software, and must not remove security software or disk encryption. Software that is needed for work but not on the approved list can be requested from IT through the service desk.

## Loss, Theft and Damage
A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to IT and the reporting manager within 24 hours. For theft, a police complaint must also be filed and a copy given to IT. IT will lock and wipe the device remotely. If the damage or loss is caused by negligence, the employee may be asked to bear part of the replacement cost, up to 25 percent. Accidental damage is reported to IT, who arrange the repair.

## Using Your Own Laptop
Personal laptops are not allowed to connect to the company network or access company data, except through the approved remote access tool and only with a manager's approval. Company files must not be copied to personal devices.

## Returning Equipment
On the last working day, employees must return the laptop, charger, access cards and any other company equipment to IT. Final settlement is released only after IT confirms that everything has been returned. A laptop that is not returned will be charged at its current book value.

## Accessories
A mouse, keyboard and headset are provided on request through the service desk. External monitors are available at the office only. Employees working from home may request an external monitor once, if their manager approves.
